Tokenisation Explainer Animation

Make a 60 to 90 second animated explainer showing how a regulated real world asset is tokenised on Redbelly through Tokeniser.

Brief

Tokenisation is Redbelly's core story, and most people outside the industry still cannot picture what it means in practice. There is no short video that walks through it from start to finish. This explainer takes one regulated asset and shows, step by step and in plain language, how it becomes a compliant token on Redbelly through Tokeniser. It will be used in Tokeniser marketing, on the DAO website and in the Start Here hub.

How it is checked: reviewers assess every submission first. The scripts of shortlisted entries are then sent to the Redbelly team for a factual check, and anything they flag comes back to you as a revision.

Scope of work
Pick one realistic regulated asset as the example, such as a property, a fund or a bond.
Write a short script that follows it from the real world asset to the token on Redbelly, using only facts from the supplied resources.
Animate the script in a clean, corporate style that looks at home next to Tokeniser's own website.
Tell the story with a voiceover, on screen text, or both, whichever suits your workflow.
Submit the script as a shared document (Google Doc, Word file or PDF) alongside the video, so it can be fact checked if your entry is shortlisted.
Deliverables
All are required
1.
The script, as a shared document (Google Doc, Word file or PDF).
2.
The final video, 60 to 90 seconds, with subtitles burned in or as a separate file. Video specs: 16:9, 1080p or higher, 24 fps or higher, MP4 or MOV.
3.
Project files from whatever tools you used.
4.
Licence proof for any music, voice, stock footage or fonts you did not make yourself.
Quality benchmarks
Someone with no crypto background understands how the asset becomes a token after one watch.
Every claim about Redbelly and Tokeniser matches the script and the supplied resources.
Looks and feels Tokeniser themed, with a corporate tone that would fit Tokeniser's own marketing.
Clean render and smooth motion, audio clear and balanced.
Failure criteria
Any one fails the submission
No script submitted, or a video that differs from its script.
Any factual claim about Redbelly or Tokeniser that is wrong or not in the supplied resources.
Price talk, investment advice or promises of returns.
Cartoon, meme or hype styling.
Any music, voice, stock asset or font without a licence that allows commercial use.
Personal credit inside the deliverable (names, handles, signatures, watermarks or "made by" lines). Contributors are credited by the DAO once the work is accepted, not inside the work itself.
